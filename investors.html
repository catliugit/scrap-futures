The system of record for unsold fashion stock.

Since 19 July 2026, large companies selling clothes or shoes in the EU, UK brands included, may not destroy unsold stock, and that includes recycling it. Exceptions need evidence and a public disclosure checked to within 10%. Today that evidence lives in emails and liquidator spreadsheets. Scrap Futures routes each unsold batch to its best legal outlet, produces the proof automatically, and turns the data into a buy-less forecast.
